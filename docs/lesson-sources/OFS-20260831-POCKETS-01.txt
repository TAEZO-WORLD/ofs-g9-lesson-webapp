Have you ever wondered why women’s clothes have fewer pockets than men’s? This question is getting a lot of attention lately, especially with the rise of the #WeWantPockets trend on social media.

Women feel frustrated that their pockets are often tiny or even fake! A recent study showed that 40% of women have picked up clothing in a store only to put it back because it didn’t have real pockets.

Fashion designers are starting to take notice. In recent shows, models strutted down the runway confidently with their hands in deep pockets. Caroline Stevenson, a fashion expert, explained, “Pockets have become a symbolic part of male and female dress.” Designers like Claire McCardell made big pockets popular in the past, knowing they help women feel more independent. Although there has been progress, the battle for pocket equality continues, and many are hopeful change is on the way!
